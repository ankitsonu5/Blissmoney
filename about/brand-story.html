Wealth should make room for life

Existing BlissMoney website imagery. People shown are illustrative.

“Money becomes meaningful through the choices it supports: time with family, independence, a business transition or a legacy you can explain.”

BlissMoney brings together two ideas that are often discussed separately. Money needs structure, discipline and attention. Life needs room for the things that make that effort worthwhile. Our proposed brand story connects those ideas through a more considered financial relationship.

That relationship starts with understanding. What responsibilities must be protected? Which goals need a deadline? What is tied up in the business or property? What would you like the next generation to understand?

These questions give a financial plan its purpose. They also create a better basis for deciding what to do next.

A practical sense of financial wellbeing

A clear purpose for every next step

We want financial conversations to be specific enough to act on and clear enough to revisit. A recommendation should be connected to a need. A question about costs or risks should receive a plain answer. A review should explain whether a change in your life calls for a change in the plan.
