Better questions make better conversations

Financial topics are easier to engage with when they start with decisions people recognise.

For an investor education session, a leadership discussion or a family wealth conversation, a useful programme gives participants a clear theme, practical questions and room for discussion.

Two formats for a focused discussion

Each session starts with decisions the people in the room already recognise, then leaves time for the questions they bring with them.

Financial clarity for leadership teams

A practical session for professionals balancing demanding roles with increasingly complex personal finances.

What the session covers

  • Understanding salary, variable pay and employer equity as one picture.
  • Linking near-term commitments with retirement and longer-term goals.
  • Keeping protection and liquidity in place through a career transition.
  • Questions to take into your next personal financial review.
Suited to internal learning sessions and leadership forums Ask about this format →

The family wealth conversation

A guided discussion for families who want more clarity on intentions, responsibilities and continuity across generations.

What the conversation covers

  • Starting with family objectives and the questions that remain unresolved.
  • Understanding ownership, access and where important records are kept.
  • Deciding when legal and tax specialists should join the conversation.
  • Leaving with a practical, agreed list of next steps.
