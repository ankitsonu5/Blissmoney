Ask about a session

Two formats for a focused discussion

Financial clarity for leadership teams

A practical session for professionals balancing demanding roles with increasingly complex personal finances.

  • • Mapping the financial effect of salary, variable pay and employer equity.
  • • Connecting near-term commitments with retirement and longer-term goals.
  • • Reviewing protection and liquidity during a career transition.
  • • Questions to take into your next personal financial review.
Suggested format: a 45-minute discussion and 15-minute Q&A. Final programme and speakers to be agreed.

The family wealth conversation

A discussion format for families who want more clarity on intentions, responsibilities and continuity.

  • • Starting with family objectives and the questions that remain unresolved.
  • • Understanding ownership, access and important records.
  • • Deciding when legal and tax specialists should join the conversation.
  • • Turning a conversation into a practical next-step list.
Suggested format: a moderated, invitation-led conversation. Scope, confidentiality and participation to be agreed.
