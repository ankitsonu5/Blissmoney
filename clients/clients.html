Bring the questions before the paperwork

Q. Do I have to move all my investments to begin?

An initial conversation is for understanding your situation. Any later engagement, onboarding or implementation depends on the scope and terms you agree.

Q. Can I enquire if my wealth is mainly in my business or property?

Yes, you can discuss the situation. Business and property values are different from readily available investment capital, so liquidity and commitments need separate attention.

Q. Does a higher portfolio value determine the right service?

Value alone does not establish suitability or service fit. Objectives, risk capacity, time horizon, complexity and the relevant engagement terms also matter.

Q. Can a family member join the discussion?

You can discuss who should participate. Sharing personal or financial information needs the appropriate consent and a clear understanding of each person's role.
