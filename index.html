Five questions before a single rupee moves.

The Five-Pillar Portfolio Framework. Every idea must answer all five — or it does not enter the portfolio.

Is the underlying business, asset or strategy sound — earnings, balance sheet, governance — without the story attached to it?

What are we paying versus what we are getting — and what has to go right for that price to make sense?

How does it behave in a drawdown? Position sizing, correlation and the exit — decided before entry, not during panic.

Where is informed money positioned — and are we early, aligned, or the liquidity someone else is waiting for?

Does price structure confirm the thesis? Entries are timed, staged and documented — never improvised.

A portfolio should be understandable in calm markets and defensible in difficult ones.
