Five questions before
a single rupee moves.

The Five-Pillar Portfolio Framework. Every idea must answer
all five before it enters the portfolio.

Is the underlying business, asset or strategy sound (earnings, balance sheet, governance) without the story attached to it?

What are we paying versus what we are getting, and what must go right for that price to make sense?

How does it behave in a drawdown? Position sizing, correlation and the exit are decided before entry, never during panic.

Where is informed money positioned, and are we early, aligned, or the liquidity someone else is waiting for?

Does price structure confirm the thesis? Entries are timed, staged and documented, never improvised.
