Time to explore what matters

A podcast can give a financial question enough space for context, examples and careful qualification. This proposed series is built around the questions clients and families might bring into a real discussion.

No podcast catalogue or recording was supplied for the preview. The first episode is therefore available as a full written concept, with no invented speaker attribution or inactive audio control.

Listen for the question behind the decision

The situation

Begin with a recognisable issue in a career, business or family financial life.

The tradeoffs

Give room to explain what is connected, what remains uncertain and where different choices have consequences.

The next conversation

End with practical questions the listener can discuss in their own context.

What deserves a longer conversation

A useful subject is often a question people find difficult to raise in a short meeting: how to discuss wealth with family, what a business exit changes or how to prepare for a new phase of life.

Send a subject suggestion or an enquiry about participation to the BlissMoney team. Guest selection, recording, editorial review and publication would need separate confirmation.
