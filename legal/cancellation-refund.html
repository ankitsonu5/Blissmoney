Cancellation & Refund Policy

How cancellation, termination and eligible refund requests are handled for fee-based services.

Regulatory DisclosuresInvestor CharterComplaints DataGrievance RedressalTerms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyRisk DisclosuresCancellation & Refund

Scope

This policy applies to fees paid directly to BlissMoney for services. It does not govern redemption, cancellation, exit load, reversal or refund relating to a third-party financial product; those are governed by the relevant issuer, intermediary and product documents.

Cancellation or termination

A client may request cancellation or termination by writing from the registered email address to contact@blissmoney.in. The effective date and consequences will be determined in accordance with the executed agreement and applicable law.

The request should include the client's name, registered contact details, service name, payment reference and reason for cancellation.

Refund assessment

  • Refund eligibility is assessed under the executed agreement, the extent of service already delivered and applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Fees attributable to services already rendered may be deducted where permitted.
  • Taxes, payment-gateway charges and third-party costs will be treated according to applicable law and the agreement.
  • No refund can reverse market loss, product charges or a transaction completed with a third-party provider.
  • An approved refund is made to the original payment source or verified client bank account.

Processing

We will acknowledge a complete request and communicate whether additional information is required. Approved refunds are initiated within a reasonable period after verification; actual credit time depends on the banking and payment systems involved.

Policy details requiring company confirmation: Any fixed cancellation window, deduction formula and processing-day commitment should be inserted only after alignment with the applicable client agreement and internal policy.

Disputes and escalation

For a concern about a cancellation or refund decision, write to compliance.officer@blissmoney.in or call +91 80020 07852. You may then follow the grievance redressal mechanism.
