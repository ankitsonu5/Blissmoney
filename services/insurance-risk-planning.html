Understand what could interrupt your plan and prepare for it.

An investment portfolio cannot answer every financial risk. Illness, loss of income, a major liability or an unexpected family event can change what your money needs to do. BlissMoney helps organise a review of your existing protection, financial responsibilities and areas of exposure. The objective is a clearer view of potential gaps and the questions to resolve with the relevant insurance providers and qualified professionals.

Protection should reflect the responsibilities you actually carry

Insurance arrangements are often added over time and left unchanged. A coordinated review considers each policy's purpose, the commitments behind it and what would happen if the expected protection were unavailable.

Your responsibilities have changed

New dependants, borrowing, care needs or a move away from employment can alter your exposure. Reassess existing policies against your present circumstances and responsibilities.

You have several policies but limited clarity

Stated cover does not explain exclusions, limits or conditions. A policy inventory helps clarify what each contract addresses and where provider confirmation is needed.

You want the family to know what to do

Accessible records, current contact details and an understanding of the insurer's process help prepare the people who may need to act. A policy alone is insufficient.

Review protection alongside your wider financial position

Income and dependant responsibilities

Consider the financial support your household relies on, outstanding liabilities and the assets available to meet obligations. Use these inputs to frame a protection discussion rather than applying a generic cover amount to everyone.

Health and existing policy review

Organise policy schedules and relevant contract information. Identify points for clarification on benefits, exclusions, waiting periods, sub-limits, continuity and claims requirements, as applicable to each policy and the insurer's current documentation.

Emergency liquidity and liability exposure

Consider how an unexpected event could affect near-term cash needs and existing obligations. Distinguish the risks potentially addressed by insurance from those requiring reserves, operational arrangements or advice from another qualified professional.

Provider and family coordination

Prepare questions for the relevant insurer or authorised intermediary, and organise the actions arising from the review. Keep family responsibilities and information access in view without representing coordination as an assurance of cover or claims acceptance.

From the question to an agreed plan

  1. Phase 01

    Understand the responsibilities

    Discuss dependants, income, borrowing and existing arrangements. Agree which areas of exposure should be considered and what information is needed for the review.

  2. Phase 02

    Organise and assess existing protection

    Build a policy inventory and compare the purpose of current cover with your financial responsibilities. Flag missing information and matters that need provider confirmation.

  3. Phase 03

    Clarify the available choices

    Coordinate questions about policy terms or potential changes with the relevant providers. Consider affordability and continuity alongside the headline premium and stated cover.

  4. Phase 04

    Keep the review current

    Revisit responsibilities and policy information after material life changes. Record renewal points and actions that require confirmation directly from the provider.

Clarity before commitment

Q.Does having employer-provided cover remove the need for a review?

Not necessarily. Consider who is covered, the terms and what happens if your employment changes. Personal protection decisions should reflect your responsibilities and the specific employer and insurer documentation.

Q.Will the review recommend cancelling existing policies?

First establish what existing policies do and what could be lost by changing them. Cancellation or replacement can affect continuity, benefits and eligibility. Discuss these consequences with the provider before acting.

Q.Can you guarantee cover or the payment of a claim?

No. Policy issuance, underwriting, exclusions and claims decisions rest with the relevant insurer under the applicable contract. A planning review or provider discussion does not establish eligibility or create a guarantee of claims acceptance.

Q.Is the lowest premium the best comparison?

Premium is one consideration. Compare the intended cover, material conditions, exclusions, limits, affordability and continuity. The relevance of each factor depends on your circumstances and the actual policy wording.

Q.What is BlissMoney's role in insurance?

The proposed service covers protection assessment and provider coordination. Any distribution, referral or servicing role must be confirmed separately. Insurance contracts are provided by insurers; clarify policy interpretation with the authorised provider.
