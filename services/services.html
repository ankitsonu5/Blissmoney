Define the scope before the work

An initial conversation should establish the decision, the work needed and the people who need to be involved.

Phase 01

Understand the context

Consider your goals, existing arrangements and the practical constraints around a decision.

Phase 02

Agree the engagement

Clarify what BlissMoney will do, what another provider will do, the relevant costs and any limitations.

Questions to ask

Q. Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Describe the decision or situation you want help with. An initial discussion can help identify the relevant scope and whether specialist input is needed.

Q. Are all services provided directly by BlissMoney?

No such assumption should be made. Mutual fund distribution is the publicly disclosed regulated activity. Legal, tax, insurance, lending, property and philanthropic work may require separate specialist or provider arrangements. Confirm the service model and engagement terms before proceeding.

Q. Can you work with my existing CA or lawyer?

Coordination with an existing professional can be discussed as part of the engagement. Responsibilities, information sharing and the work to be delivered need to be agreed by the parties involved.

Q. How are fees or commissions handled?

Ask for a written explanation of the relevant charges, commissions, referral arrangements and provider costs before committing. This preview does not state a fee schedule or a fee-only advisory status.
